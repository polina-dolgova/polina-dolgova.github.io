Polina Dolgova

Hi! I am a first-year PhD student at CISPA in Germany, working with Sebastian U. Stich.

📐 Prior to my PhD, I studied both pure mathematics (during my bachelor’s at HSE in Moscow) and computer science (master’s at HSE, Yandex School of Data Analysis). This background strongly shaped my interest in mathematical explanations and theoretical motivations behind learning algorithms :)

🏭 I also spent several years in industry: working with LLMs at Yandex (russian bigtech) and on audio/CV problems in a pharma R&D team in Spain.

🧠 My research interests center on machine unlearning. I’m particularly interested in how to define and reason about forgetting in modern models, and in the analysis of unlearning algorithms. More broadly, I enjoy problems where mathematical structure helps clarify how learning algorithms behave.

research interests

  • Machine unlearning, including certified guarantees
  • Stability and efficiency of learning algorithms
  • Mathematical foundations of modern machine learning
  • Bridging theory and practice in machine learning
